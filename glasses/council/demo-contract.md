## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement the following expressions shall have the following meanings:

"**Album**" means a Record embodying not fewer than ten (10) Masters of different
Compositions, each not less than two minutes thirty seconds in duration, having an aggregate
playing time of not less than thirty-five (35) minutes, which is technically and commercially
satisfactory to the Company in its sole discretion;

"**Ancillary Income**" means all income of whatever nature arising from the Artist's
activities in the entertainment industry other than income from the exploitation of Masters,
including without limitation live performance, touring, merchandising, sponsorship,
endorsement, brand partnership, fan-club, acting, modelling, literary, broadcasting, social
media and personal appearance income;

"**Compositions**" means all musical compositions and lyrics (or any part thereof) written,
co-written, composed, arranged or adapted by the Artist, whether alone or with others,
during the Term or embodied in any Master;

"**Contract Period**" means the Initial Period and each Option Period;

"**Gross Receipts**" means all monies actually received by or credited to the Company, the
Artist or any person on their behalf, before deduction of any costs, commissions, taxes or
expenses;

"**Initial Period**" means the period commencing on the date of this Agreement and expiring
twelve (12) months after the date of Delivery of the second Album recorded hereunder;

"**Masters**" means every recording of sound, whether or not coupled with a visual image,
embodying the performances of the Artist made during the Term or otherwise owned or
controlled by the Artist at the date hereof, including outtakes, demos, stems, rehearsals,
live recordings and remixes;

"**Net Receipts**" means Gross Receipts from the exploitation of Masters less the Deductions
set out in Clause 7.3;

"**Option Period**" means each of the further Contract Periods described in Clause 3.2;

"**Professional Name**" means the name "ARU" and any other name, logo, device or mark under
which the Artist performs or is known during the Term, together with all goodwill therein;

"**Recording Costs**" means all costs incurred by the Company in connection with the
production of Masters and Videos, including studio hire, engineers, producers, mixers,
session musicians, equipment, travel, accommodation, subsistence, per diems, mastering,
artwork, legal fees of the Company in connection with clearances, and an administration
charge of fifteen per cent (15%) of the foregoing;

"**Record**" means any device, format or medium, now known or hereafter devised, by which
sound (with or without images) may be reproduced, transmitted or made available;

"**Territory**" means the Universe;

"**Term**" means the period commencing on the date hereof and continuing until the expiry of
the last Contract Period, or until terminated in accordance with Clause 13;

"**Video**" means any audiovisual recording embodying a Master.

1.2 Clause headings are for convenience only. Words in the singular include the plural and
vice versa. "Including" means "including without limitation".

## 2. ENGAGEMENT AND EXCLUSIVITY

2.1 The Company hereby engages the Artist, and the Artist agrees, to render her exclusive
services as a recording artist, performer and songwriter to the Company throughout the Term
and Territory.

2.2 During the Term the Artist shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company
(which may be withheld in the Company's absolute discretion):

(a) render any recording, performing, songwriting or producing services to any third party;

(b) authorise the use of her name, Professional Name, likeness, voice or biographical
material in connection with any Record or audiovisual product not released by the Company;

(c) record, re-record or perform for the purpose of recording any Composition recorded
hereunder;

(d) enter into any agreement with any manager, agent, publisher, label or distributor.

2.3 The Artist acknowledges that her services are unique and extraordinary and that any
breach of Clause 2.2 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which damages would be an
inadequate remedy, and the Company shall accordingly be entitled to injunctive relief.

---

## 3. TERM AND OPTIONS

3.1 The Term shall commence on the date of this Agreement and continue for the Initial
Period.

3.2 The Company shall have five (5) separate and successive irrevocable options, each
exercisable by the Company alone, to extend the Term for further Option Periods. Each Option
Period shall commence on the expiry of the preceding Contract Period and shall expire twelve
(12) months after the date of Delivery of the last Album required to be delivered in that
Option Period.

3.3 Each option shall be exercised by written notice to the Artist at any time prior to the
expiry of the then-current Contract Period. If the Company fails to give such notice, the
then-current Contract Period shall be deemed extended until thirty (30) days after the Artist
has served written notice on the Company requesting that the Company exercise or decline the
option.

3.4 The Artist shall have no right to terminate or shorten the Term or any Contract Period.

3.5 For the avoidance of doubt, periods during which the Artist is in breach, is
unavailable, is ill or is engaged in activities not approved by the Company shall be added to
the then-current Contract Period and the Term shall be extended accordingly.

---

## 4. MINIMUM COMMITMENT AND DELIVERY

4.1 During the Initial Period the Artist shall record and Deliver to the Company two (2)
Albums. During each Option Period the Artist shall record and Deliver one (1) Album, together
with not fewer than four (4) additional Masters for single, EP or promotional release.

4.2 The Artist shall Deliver the first Album within nine (9) months of the date hereof and
each subsequent Album within nine (9) months of the Company's written request.

4.3 "**Delivery**" shall occur only when the Company has given written notice that it has
received and accepted fully mixed, edited, mastered Masters, together with all consents,
clearances, label copy, lyric sheets, split sheets, session musician forms and artwork, as
technically and commercially satisfactory to the Company. The Company's acceptance shall not
be unreasonably delayed beyond ninety (90) days from receipt.

4.4 Nothing in this Agreement shall oblige the Company to record, produce, complete, release,
distribute, promote or otherwise exploit any Master, Album or Video, or to provide any studio
time, producer, budget or personnel, and the Company's decisions in such matters shall be
final. The Company's sole obligation in respect of Recordings shall be to account for
Royalties in accordance with Clause 7 in respect of Masters actually exploited.

4.5 The Artist shall attend all recording sessions, rehearsals, photo shoots, interviews,
promotional appearances and performances scheduled by the Company on reasonable notice, at
her own expense save as otherwise agreed in writing.

## 6. ADVANCES

6.1 Subject to the Artist's full performance of this Agreement, the Company shall pay the
Artist the following advances, each of which shall be fully recoupable from any and all sums
payable to the Artist hereunder:

(a) on signature: £7,500;

(b) on Delivery of the first Album: £7,500;

(c) on the commencement of each Option Period: an amount equal to two-thirds (2/3) of the
Royalties credited to the Artist's account in respect of the immediately preceding Album
during the twelve (12) months following its release, subject to a minimum of £5,000 and a
maximum of £20,000.

6.2 All Recording Costs, Video costs, independent promotion, marketing, tour support, travel,
styling, legal and accounting fees incurred by the Company in connection with the Artist, and
any other sums paid by the Company to or on behalf of the Artist, shall be deemed additional
advances recoupable from Royalties and from Ancillary Income.

## 7. ROYALTIES AND ACCOUNTING

7.1 Subject to recoupment of all advances, the Company shall credit to the Artist's account a
royalty (the "**Royalty**") of fifteen per cent (15%) of Net Receipts derived by the Company
from the exploitation of the Masters in the Territory.

7.2 Until all advances and recoupable costs have been recouped, the Company shall be
entitled to retain one hundred per cent (100%) of the Artist's Royalties. Following
recoupment, the Company shall be entitled to retain eighty-five per cent (85%) of Net
Receipts and shall pay the balance to the Artist.

7.3 "**Deductions**" means: (a) a packaging and container charge of twenty-five per cent
(25%) of the applicable wholesale price on all physical Records; (b) a reduction of fifty per
cent (50%) in respect of Records sold or made available by any digital, streaming,
subscription, bundled or "new technology" means; (c) free goods, promotional copies and
returns, which shall be excluded from Gross Receipts; (d) all distribution fees, aggregator
fees and third-party commissions; (e) VAT and all taxes and withholdings; (f) a reserve
against returns and credits of thirty-five per cent (35%), liquidated within four (4)
accounting periods; (g) all Recording Costs and recoupable sums under Clause 6.2.

7.4 All advances, Recording Costs and recoupable sums under this Agreement shall be
cross-collateralised, such that any unrecouped balance in respect of any Master, Album, Video
or Contract Period may be recouped from Royalties, Ancillary Income, publishing income and
any other sums whatsoever payable to the Artist under this or any other agreement with the
Company or its affiliates.

7.5 The Company shall render statements semi-annually within ninety (90) days of 30 June and
31 December in respect of sums actually received by the Company during the preceding
accounting period, and shall pay any balance shown due together with such statement. The
Company may withhold payment of any balance below £250.

7.6 Each statement shall be conclusively binding upon the Artist unless the Artist gives
written notice of specific objection within twelve (12) months of the date of the statement.
The Artist may, not more than once in any period of three (3) years and at her own cost, by a
certified accountant not engaged on a contingency basis and on not less than sixty (60) days'
written notice, inspect the Company's books relating to the immediately preceding two (2)
accounting periods only.

## 8. MECHANICAL ROYALTIES — CONTROLLED COMPOSITIONS

8.1 The Artist hereby grants to the Company an irrevocable licence to reproduce the
Compositions in Records and to make them available by any means.

8.2 In respect of each Composition embodied in a Master, the Company shall pay (or cause to
be paid) a mechanical royalty equal to seventy-five per cent (75%) of the minimum rate from
time to time applicable under the relevant collecting society scheme or statutory licence in
the country of sale (the "**Controlled Rate**"), without regard to playing time, and subject
to a maximum aggregate mechanical royalty per Album equivalent to ten (10) times the
Controlled Rate regardless of the number of Compositions embodied therein. The Controlled
Rate shall apply to all formats including streaming and downloads.

8.3 No mechanical royalties shall be payable in respect of promotional copies, free goods,
Records sold below wholesale price, or Records for which the Company does not receive
payment, and any mechanical royalties payable to third-party co-writers in excess of the
Controlled Rate shall be deducted from the Artist's Royalties.

## 9. ANCILLARY INCOME

9.1 In consideration of the Company's investment in the Artist's career and brand, the
Artist shall pay to the Company fifty per cent (50%) of all Gross Receipts from Ancillary

the Company has procured, negotiated or contributed to the relevant activity.

9.2 The Artist shall procure that all contracts generating Ancillary Income direct payment
of the Company's share to the Company at source, and shall provide the Company with copies of
all such contracts and statements within fourteen (14) days of receipt.

9.3 The Company's share of Ancillary Income shall be payable in addition to, and shall not
be credited against, the recoupment of advances.

13.2 The Company may suspend its obligations and extend the Term for the duration of any

## 14. POST-TERM RESTRICTIONS

14.1 For a period of thirty-six (36) months following the expiry or termination of the Term
the Artist shall not render recording services to, or record any Record for, any third party,
nor perform live under any name other than one approved in writing by the Company.

14.2 For a period of fifteen (15) years following the expiry or termination of the Term the
Artist shall not record, re-record, perform for the purpose of recording, or authorise the
recording of any Composition recorded hereunder or any Composition written during the Term.

14.3 The Artist acknowledges that the restrictions in this Clause 14 are reasonable and
necessary to protect the Company's legitimate interests in the Masters and the Professional
Name.
