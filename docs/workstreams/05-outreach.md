# 05. Outreach

## Why it matters
A community service has impact only if musicians find it. This workstream took Souls of Muse to public channels and designed regional outreach.

### Growth plan (sanitized summary)
1. A person reads the Convention, signs, and receives a card with their name written as music (see [04](04-community-and-governance.md)).
2. Early signatories become "First Keepers"; ambassadors follow.
3. Paid promotion, if any, only amplifies what already works organically.

Owner rules (31 Oct 2026): signing is always free and voluntary; where a signature came from is never recorded or shown; posts are in English or in the language of the country being addressed, with one caption for all roles. A signature log keyed by platform and account, with repeats marked, was prepared for counting.

### Regional outreach (sanitized summary)
Idea of the project owner: invite 10–15 live-music musicians in Japan and South Korea by sending each one their own name as a notation signature, written with the native grid (Hangul consonants or the iroha poem), as a gift with a polite invitation and no request to repost. Channels considered: KakaoTalk and LINE, leading to the community's Meta pages. Status: plan under review; no musician has been contacted and no names are published.

## Paths that did not work
- **Signing by comment.** "Comment I sign under the pinned post" counted as a signature from 29 Oct 2026. By 1 Nov signers had not received the promised notation-card reply and the profile link to the site did not open; the owner dropped the method on 2 Nov in favour of an explicit "Sign the Convention" button on the site.
- **A third Meta AI caption session** failed and its output was not used.
- **Notation cards for Devanagari and Tamil names.** Names written in these scripts produce no notes in the current engine, so the name-as-music gift that the plan relies on cannot yet be made for Hindi- or Tamil-script names, even though invitations in Hindi were prepared (see [04](04-community-and-governance.md)).
- **Paid promotion** did not run before submission; growth so far is organic only.
- **Educational shorts** were approved for design on 2 Nov but were never posted to Facebook or Instagram before submission.
