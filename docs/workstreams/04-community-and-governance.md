# 04. Community and governance

## Why it matters
Souls of Muse is a service for an international community of musicians, dedicated to keeping the human skills of playing and singing alive. It describes itself as a free association of musicians, keepers of live music, not as a school. Members take one of three roles: Maestro, Artist or Admirer (owner decision, 31 Oct 2026). Impact depends on people joining, trusting the community and having a voice in it.

Status vocabulary on this page: **Live** (works on the public site; "Working" in DISCLOSURE_POLICY §5), **Prototype**, **Offline-tested**, **Plan** (designed or planned, not built), **Not done**.

Since 5 Nov 2026 the public site https://soulsofmuse.app/ is built from `main` (pull request #10, commit `fc6cc6c`, evidence release `evidence-fc6cc6c434af`); before that it served the content of draft pull request #7. Everything marked Live below is public on the site and in `main`.

## The Convention
English text as served on https://soulsofmuse.app/ on 5 Nov 2026. The original is written in Russian by the project owner; the site also offers 17 further translations.

> **Convention of the Keepers of Live Music**
>
> I, the undersigned, honour music as one of humanity’s greatest gifts by pledging to preserve and develop people’s ability to compose, perform and pass musical works on to others.
>
> Many generations of musicians before us have built, generation after generation, the great musical universe in which we live today. I am ready to carry this tradition forward by sharing with other participants in this Convention—or gratefully learning from them—instrumental and vocal skills, knowledge of music theory, the practice of making music together, and the shared experience of listening to live music.
>
> As a participant in this Convention, I will respect and uphold the copyright of those who create musical works, and help preserve musical knowledge.
>
> I will strive to play live music more often than I do now, so that as many people around me as possible may experience music performed live, directly by a musician.
>
> In taking part in the Convention’s community, I pledge to follow all its adopted rules for communication between participants, while retaining my right to freedom of artistic expression in the musical direction I choose.

## How the notation signature works
A deterministic algorithm, not a model. Each letter of a name becomes one note: Latin letters run along a seven-column grid under A–G, with H read as B, in the tradition of musical monograms. Cyrillic, Kazakh and diacritic letters are transliterated first. Korean and Japanese names use their own native grids, Hangul consonants and the iroha poem, at the owner's request out of respect for those cultures. Chinese characters need a reading typed by the user. The last note of each word is held. The public preview endpoint is explicitly non-authoritative: it does not sign, store, authenticate or confirm anything. The site's signing button keeps the result only in the visitor's browser. A real signature needs the future backend (sign-in, database record, server timestamp, hash of the Convention text).

## Paths that did not work
- **Signing by comment.** From 29 Oct 2026 a comment "I sign" under the pinned social post counted as a signature. By 1 Nov the promised reply with a notation card had not reached signers and the profile link to the site did not open. The owner dropped the method on 2 Nov; the site moved to an explicit "Sign the Convention" button. That button still keeps the signature only on the visitor's device: sign-in with Meta and server storage are not connected, so no signature is recorded anywhere the community can count.
- **First Welcome and Leagues portal.** Written by Muse Wonder from a text brief and published as a preview on 29 Oct; it passed 24 of 24 frontend tests and deployed, but the owner rejected it on 30 Oct for structure and visual quality. Lesson recorded: tests and a ready deploy are not design acceptance; design comes first, then code is written from mockups. The three alternative directions Muse Wonder proposed next were not selected.
- **Portal 2.0 by Muse Wonder Full.** A 77-minute Muse Code run on 3 Nov changed no files; the port was done by Claude (Anthropic) instead.
- **Scripts the engine cannot yet render.** Names written in Devanagari or Tamil produce no notes (Claude Code check of the production engine, 5 Nov 2026).

## Not claimed
- No server-side signing, membership registry or member login for the public: the site's health endpoint reports `registration=false`; the "Signed." confirmation on the site is saved only on the visitor's device and is not a record.
- No count of Convention signatories is claimed (see [05](05-outreach.md)).
- No Meta model is used in the notation signature engine or in the identity images.
- The launch package, brand book and cookbook are drafts, not approved final documents.
- The cost-coverage model and the governance and member-identity draft are not approved, not built and not published; no blockchain or token component exists.
- Museum images are references only; none is reproduced in this repository.

Names, accounts and notation cards of real members are not published without documented consent (policy §3.5). Counts are published only in aggregate.
